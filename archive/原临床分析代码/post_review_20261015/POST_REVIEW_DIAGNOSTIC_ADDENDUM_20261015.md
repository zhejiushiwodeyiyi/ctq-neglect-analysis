# Diagnostic addendum, 15 October 2026

This addendum was written **after inspection of the first post-review diagnostic results**. The initial all-linear neglect Q25-to-Q75 general-distress contrast was 0.17044 SD, whereas the jointly flexible exposure-and-baseline-distress specification gave 0.00017 SD. These results prompted this bounded clarification. This is neither the original analysis plan nor preregistration, and the new calculations are not independent replication.

Retain the original data, first plan, script, 20-model manifest and every existing result unchanged and record their SHA-256 hashes. Use the same corrected de-identified 0–3 questionnaire table, full-wave-2 N = 8,902 scaling reference, paired N = 4,219 and 10 schools. Do not repeat item recoding or rerun the original content models or bootstrap tests.

Fit exactly two additional general-distress specifications: (1) natural cubic splines for both CTQ composites and a linear baseline-distress term; (2) linear CTQ composites and a natural cubic spline for baseline distress. Use exactly the already fixed spline knots/boundaries and three centered degrees of freedom per flexible term, both composites, grade-by-sex terms and school fixed effects. Report the same full-baseline Q25-to-Q75 conditional prediction differences, CR1 school standard errors and t(9) intervals. No new p values, multiplicity claims, knot searches or specification selection will be used.

For a diagnostic graph, also reconstruct the previously fitted complete-spline general-distress model once because only its IQR contrasts, not its coefficient vector/covariance, were stored. Require reconstructed IQR estimates, standard errors and confidence limits to agree with the frozen first diagnostic output within 1e-10. This is a necessary computational reconstruction, not an additional independent confirmation. Its fit appears separately in the addendum manifest. The all-linear curve can be recovered exactly from its frozen IQR contrast and standard error without refitting.

For all four specifications, calculate the conditional difference in predicted general-distress SD units between each integer neglect score from 10 through the full-wave-2 empirical P95 (rounded down only for the integer grid) and the reference score 10, averaging the corresponding prediction differences over the matched participants' covariates. Use a single design-vector difference and its full school covariance for each interval, so the reference point has estimate and interval exactly zero. These are model-based mean differences conditional on the observed adjustment set, not causal interventions or validated individual predictions. They are pointwise, not simultaneous, confidence intervals.

Output a full-wave-2 aggregate frequency distribution for integer neglect scores and record Q25, Q75, P95, N, grid maximum, exact knot provenance, execution versions and input/output hashes. Do not export participant identifiers, individual fitted values or observations. Describe how the two specified changes alter the estimate; do not interpret such comparison as a causal attribution of bias to one variable.
